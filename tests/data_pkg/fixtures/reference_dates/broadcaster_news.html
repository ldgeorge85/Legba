Leaders condemn attack on checkpost

September 26, 2026

(File Photo)

The head of state and the prime minister have strongly condemned the attack on a police checkpost in the Example district.

In their separate statements, they expressed deep grief and sorrow over the loss of lives in the incident.

They offered condolences to the bereaved families and directed the relevant institutions to provide medical facilities to the injured.

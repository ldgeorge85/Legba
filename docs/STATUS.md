# Status — the truth-in-labeling page

What is real, what is gated, what is only designed — on one page. A capability
is either **built** (runs end-to-end today), a **guarded seam** (the surface
exists but refuses activation / raises loudly until its real edge is wired —
never a silent stub), or **designed, not built** (the design is written in
[DIRECTION.md](DIRECTION.md); no code claims it). The authoritative seam
registry is [SEAMS.md](SEAMS.md); per-route/per-panel maturity is in
[RELEASE_STATE_MATRIX.md](RELEASE_STATE_MATRIX.md).

## Where it is weak today (read this first)

Live and source-first — **single-operator / single-tenant**, single-node,
run-it-yourself. The analysis spine runs end-to-end: catalog sources flow
through enrichment and fan-out; the nine bounded units write `[N]`-cited
findings that pass the mandatory faithfulness verify; the composition tower
(`country_composition` → `region_composition` → `world_assessor`, plus the
thematic `escalation_composition`) composes the **verified** sub-claims into
hedged per-country, per-region, and world reads; `scorecard_producer` writes
one banded row per active desk. Cold-start from empty volumes through to a
verified scorecard is proven from a single baseline schema migration.

And, plainly:

- The composition-tier reads have been externally graded **twice at ~0.48
  accuracy** (R2 2026-08-25 on a valid instrument; R3 2026-08-29 concurring,
  instrument-limited) against a pre-committed 0.75 bar — so the layer is
  **ruled for demotion** to quotation-constrained assembly (design complete,
  build pending). Until that lands, treat composed prose as a *pointer to the
  desk findings it cites*, not as an independently trustworthy synthesis; the
  per-claim citations and verify verdicts are the trustworthy grain.
- A single-frame country desk can be structurally **blind to its second
  story** (measured: the IL desks never engaged the Iran war though the
  evidence sat in every slice — frame monopoly steers attention). A coverage
  census + floor detector is queued; until then, desk *silence* on a topic is
  weak evidence of absence.
- The scorecard is a **mix** — some desks band from verified claims, others
  read all-`insufficient-evidence` (e.g. a desk whose unit faithfulness is
  genuinely low); the scorecard refuses to fabricate a band.
- The correctness-vs-reference gold set is **small-sample** (one labeled
  cohort). A weekly stratified labeling loop grows the correctness figure; its
  first cohort (2026-W31, n=8) is labeled, and since 2026-08-03 that cohort's
  arithmetic actually reaches the readouts: one shared module
  (`correctness_axis`) feeds the unit scorer, the eval scoreboard, the
  scorecard fold, `GET /v3/eval/correctness`, and GEPA's gate — the scorer had
  been reading a dead reference table with one stale row (that deterministic
  reference leg is still honestly n=1/unmeasured) — displayed as its own axis
  with tiny-n labeling and structurally barred from pooling into faithfulness
  calibration. Labels come
  from **outside the production plane** — the operator, or an out-of-plane
  frontier model grounding each verdict in live web sources — with the labeler
  identity stamped on every label (`labeled_by`). That out-of-plane property is
  **operational discipline, not a code-enforced invariant**: the stamp is
  recorded, never validated. And n=8 is still a small sample.
- **Faithfulness cannot see a faithful-but-wrong claim**, and one week's
  gold-set cohort measured how often that bites: 5 of 8 downgrades were an
  absence stated as a world fact by a desk whose sources simply carried
  nothing. A deterministic backstop and a prompt rule on all 11 inline units
  now push those toward collection-scoped phrasing — but scoping the *claim*
  is not the same as checking it against the *world*, and no leg of the verify
  pass does the latter.
- **The coherence loop flags; it does not correct.** New evidence that bears
  on a standing open question lands a review flag and an append-only edge —
  and stops there. No correction propagates back into the product that rested
  on the stale reading, and nothing ever closes an open question. The debt
  count is readable (`GET /v3/system/staleness-debt`) and says so on the wire:
  `match_verified: false` (SEAMS #49). The matcher's precision was measured
  out-of-plane across four rounds: the first (2026-07-29; 122 labeled pairs)
  put **pooled pairwise precision at 0.279** against a recommended ≥0.85 bar,
  and two trains of measured levers later, round 4 measured the live
  bearing-gated `claim_watch/4.0.0` stream at **0.908** — the bar is met on
  the gated stream. The closer still does not exist in-tree; building it is a
  held operator decision, not a consequence the measurement triggers on its
  own. The round-by-round history is in the `claim_watch` row below.
- The acute-forecast pilot reports **no proven skill** and abstains on
  degenerate windows. Its resolution path is real now — the first exogenously
  graded windows landed 2026-08, with degenerate windows **voided** (counted
  as drained work, never graded) and graded ones scored separately — but the
  graded sample is far too small for any skill claim.
- The GEPA `unit_optimizer` measures a real faithfulness delta that is not yet
  positive (a recent cycle: `0.34 → 0.29`), so it promotes nothing.
- The ACH / calibration layer is built and traceable but carries **no
  validated skill metric** — every resolved outcome to date is the
  self-consistency tier, not an exogenous real-world resolution.
- The faithfulness judge on the reference deployment now runs
  **cross-family**: `LEGBA_JUDGE_STACK_REF` — rung 1 of the judge-resolution
  ladder (`LEGBA_JUDGE_STACK_REF` > `method.llm.judge` > `.verify` >
  `.primary`) — repoints every judge call at a hosted Gemma endpoint
  (Cerebras), a different model family from the core plane that writes the
  prose. The **shipped descriptor default remains same-model**
  (self-hostable): [RELEASE_STATE.md](RELEASE_STATE.md) derives that verdict
  mechanically (25 of 25 verify-bearing analysts' descriptors read
  `SAME-MODEL`) and now reports **both layers** — the descriptor default and
  the effective env-repointed route — after an earlier revision printed
  "SAME-MODEL" while the live rig judged cross-family. The route was
  validated before it was adopted: a bounded day-trial, then an out-of-plane
  adjudication (two independent panels re-graded 53 sampled claims against
  their actual cited evidence) that found both judges safe on passes and
  over-fired on failures, with the largest verdict errors **structural scope
  bugs in the verify path itself** — since fixed across stamped judge
  pipeline revisions (every critique carries `judge_pipeline_version`, now
  `2026-08-10/1`, so pre/post populations never pool). Honest residue of the
  cross-family gain: a hosted judge can be **down** — a ~30-hour billing-wall
  outage stamped every critique in its window `judge_status='deterministic'`
  (the citation-floor fallback, published **PROVISIONAL** under a 0.85
  ceiling, never a fabricated pass), and a `judge_availability` gauge now
  replays exactly that outage shape as critical-and-paging. Pointing the
  judge back at the local plane is one config line, at the cost of returning
  to same-model grading; a self-hosted cross-family judge when GPU capacity
  allows remains the end-state.
- The `world_context` vector RAG is a **guarded, measured pilot** on a single
  unit (`internal_stability`) — firing it has historically thickened the
  low-faithfulness tail, so a per-run auto-rollback guard reverts injection the
  moment that recurs; `leadership_transition` RAG is rolled back and OFF.
- Most of the 2026-07 additions ship **honestly gated**: the new deterministic
  analysts register as `draft` descriptors (an operator activates them), and
  every behavior-changing consumption path sits behind a flag that defaults
  **OFF in code** — two-tier composition, structural-verify gating, fact-decay
  weighting, earned-weight tie-breaks, the LLM contention tie-break, outward
  alert delivery. The per-row notes below name each gate; a row saying
  "built" with a default-OFF flag means the compute runs and is visible, the
  consequence does not engage until the operator opts in. Tree `draft` + live
  `active` is the **designed lifecycle** (activation is a live operator act
  through the audited FSM route; the state-drift gauge reports those rows as
  expected promotions, never as drift), and most of the once-`draft` analysts
  below — the alert trigger scan, `claim_watch`, the evidence archiver, the
  calibration/baseline/narrative/track-record set — have since been activated
  on the reference deployment.

**No enterprise, multi-tenant, RBAC, or forecast-accuracy / Brier-skill claim
is made.**

## Retirements & freezes

Sequenced and documented in [SEAMS.md](SEAMS.md), so the trusted spine stays
clean:

- **`country_assessor` (monolithic per-country one-pager) — RETIRED /
  STOPPED.** The nine units + composition supersede it; nothing in the spine
  reads it. Its ~1.2k historical findings remain in the DB, unread — not
  deleted, not a clean slate.
- **`country_predictor`, `india_energy_predictor` (forecast-as-claim) —
  RETIRED / STOPPED.** Forecasting returns only as the measured
  `acute_forecasts` Brier/BSS scoreboard, never a free-text claim; ~539
  historical prediction rows remain in the DB.
- **`country_optimizer` (monolithic GEPA) — cadence-FROZEN** (descriptor still
  `state=active`). GEPA returns only as the scoped, faithfulness-measured
  `unit_optimizer`.
- **`cross_correlator` (cross-analyst correlation) — RETIRED / STOPPED**
  (2026-07). Its correlation / contradiction-surfacing mission is carried by
  the contested-claims arbiter (detect-only contention records over the fact
  pool); ~49 historical findings remain in the DB, unread.
- **`journal_assessor` — RUNS (on cadence).** An off-chain voice roster that
  writes only `journal_entries`, never product output: the 12-hour entry +
  daily consolidator, the weekly third-person chronicle, four weekly faculty
  lenses (one falsifiable prior each), and the weekly `lens_diff` chorus pass.
- **`world_assessor` — NOT retired.** It graduated into the world composition
  (repointed onto `meta_findings_synthesizer`).

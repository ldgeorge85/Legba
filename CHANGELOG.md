# Changelog

What changed in each release, for people running or evaluating Legba. Newest first. Each release
is a fresh-volume install: there is no migration path from an earlier design of the platform.

## Deprecated

- `GET /api/v1/source_credibility`, `GET /api/v1/source_credibility/{host}` and
  `GET /api/v1/sources/{source_id}/assurance` passed their sunset date (2026-10-27). They still
  answer, with `Deprecation` and `Sunset` headers. Use `GET /api/v1/v3/source-quality` and
  `GET /api/v1/v3/sources/{source_id}/quality`.

## 2026-11

Accounts and roles:

- Sign-in with local accounts, behind `LEGBA_AUTH_SIGNIN=1` (off by default). Three roles
  (reader, contributor, admin) and one owner account. Every API route has a minimum role; see
  [docs/API_ROLES.md](docs/API_ROLES.md).
- With sign-in on, `LEGBA_EDGE_PERIMETER=0` drops the edge's perimeter password.
- Every row that records a judgement or a change names who wrote it: a person, a service, an
  analyst or a rule.

Console:

- The console was rebuilt around four reader pages (Board, Subject, Ledger, Watch) and a separate
  operator side. Consult sits under the main reading area and can be moved or collapsed.
- A phone shell at `/m/`. With sign-in on it can be installed from the phone's browser.
- Alerts can be acknowledged and snoozed by a contributor or an admin.
- Forecast outcomes are decided in a resolution queue on the operator side.

Record and provenance:

- Citations are stored as rows beside each finding. `scripts/citation_rows_backfill.py` converts
  findings written before this release (dry run by default).
- A held record of statements with a hash-chained status log, off by default
  (`LEGBA_HELD_RECORD_SHADOW`).
- The forecast ledger gained a resolver per question kind and a scorecard that shows no score until
  five questions have resolved.
- The empty Apache AGE graph database was removed. The typed graph is a projection into ordinary
  tables (`LEGBA_GRAPH_PROJECTION`).

Sources:

- Claim sources: a source that may only support "X claimed Y". Each one needs a recorded sign-off
  before it can go active.
- An on-request fetch intake (`LEGBA_INTAKE_DRAIN`) and a server-sent-events subscribe connector
  (`LEGBA_SUBSCRIBE_SUPERVISOR`), both off by default.
- The evidence archive can move older objects into a cold tier (`scripts/archive_tier.py`).
- The `email_imap` source kind.

Operations:

- Instance profiles: `profiles/<id>/profile.yaml` selects the descriptors, rosters, prompts and
  console labels for an instance. Two ship: `geopolitics` (the example instance) and a
  `sanctions_export_control` skeleton.
- One registrar, `scripts/bringup_register_descriptors.py`, registers every descriptor at deploy.
- `scripts/rolling_deploy.sh --tier` picks the test gate a change needs and deploys it.
- `scripts/fleet_stop.sh` stops the two processes that call models and leaves the rest running.
- `LEGBA_REGISTRY_WORKERS` runs the registry with more than one worker process.
- No output-token cap is sent to any model route unless the route requires one.
- List routes (`/findings`, `/signals` and others) return summary rows unless `fields=full` is
  asked for.
- FastAPI is pinned to one version across the images.

## 2026-09

- Typed absence: `GET /api/v1/v3/absence?scope=<subject>` says what kind of gap a subject has,
  with a reason, and the reader pages show it.
- Curated collections of past observations, kept apart from the live record.
- A contrary-evidence pass that searches for material against a claim and records a contention.
- The correctness grader publishes its coverage beside its score
  (`GET /api/v1/v3/eval/grader_roster`).
- Events with an append-only transition ledger and as-of reads, behind `LEGBA_EVENTS`.
- A standing external auditor that checks sampled claims against web search.
- A read-telemetry ledger and a read scoreboard panel.
- Alert paging limits: a daily page budget and a guard against repeated pages for an unchanged
  state.

## 2026-08

- A CI workflow (lint and structural gates) and a nightly test suite.
- A deploy smoke step that runs one analyst from cold after every deploy.
- The WebSocket credential moved from a query parameter to a subprotocol.
- `docs/RELEASE_STATE.md` is generated by a script; counts are not typed by hand.

## 2026-06

- First public release, under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
